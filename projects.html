01 / EMBEDDED SYSTEMS + POWER ELECTRONICS

ChantSat-1 3U CubeSat Demonstrator

Project overview

Led the end-to-end development of the first physical model created for Coastal Carolina University’s 3U CubeSat program. The project began with basic Arduino experience and progressed through system architecture, breadboard validation, perfboard integration, functional testing, and public demonstration.

Integrated functions

  • Switchable solar-panel and external wall-power operation
  • Rechargeable battery and MPPT charge controller
  • Voltage and current monitoring with telemetry displays
  • MicroSD data logging
  • ESP32 Bluetooth image reception
  • TFT image display simulating a satellite “selfie stick” payload

02 / MECHANICAL DESIGN + MANUFACTURING TOOLING

Laser Engraver Fixture

Project overview

Designed and constructed an adjustable laser engraver fixture using custom 3D-printed components, metal guide rods, bearings, and threaded adjustment hardware. The design progressed from a hand sketch to CAD, a functional first build, and an improved revision.

Iteration highlights

  • Vertical adjustment through a lead screw and handwheel
  • Parallel guide rods and bearing-supported motion
  • Horizontal extension for laser-head positioning
  • Revised printed geometry after prototype evaluation
  • Added shield around the laser head to reduce stray exposure to nearby personnel
